Company

Built by people who lost weeks to a bad model

Kestrel RF Systems started in 2016 in a rented corner of an anechoic chamber in Boulder, Colorado, after our founders spent a program arguing with a spreadsheet that turned out to be wrong about a diffraction edge.

What we believe

RF engineering has excellent physics and terrible tooling. The models are decades of solid work; the software around them is a pile of scripts, license dongles, and file formats nobody documented.

We think the fix is boring and structural: put the physics behind a clean API, make every result reproducible by construction, and refuse to build anything that only works through a GUI. If an engineer cannot script it, version it, and diff it, we have not finished the feature.

That principle costs us some flashy demos. It has also meant that customers who bought Studio in 2018 can still rerun those exact results today, byte for byte, on a machine that did not exist back then.

How we work

Four commitments we have not broken yet

Engineers answer support

Tier one is staffed by people who write the solver. There is no script to escape from.

Published validation

Every solver release ships a measurement comparison against reference ranges. Including where we disagree.

No usage metering

Run as many simulations, sweeps, and CI jobs as you like. We price seats, not cycles.

Your data stays yours

No telemetry, no phone-home licensing, no scenario uploads. Air-gapped installs are a supported path, not an exception.
