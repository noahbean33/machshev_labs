Tier 3 · compliance navigation Available now

FCC/CE pre-compliance & certification navigation

Guidance from bench pre-compliance testing through formal sign-off at an accredited lab. If you have already failed, this is the fastest route back — we correlate the lab's own scan data to a root cause instead of guessing.

Before your first submission

  • Work out which standards actually apply to your device and market
  • Bench pre-compliance so you are not paying lab rates to find obvious problems
  • Stackup and enclosure review with emissions in mind, not just manufacturability
  • Choosing and briefing a lab, and reading the quote they send you

After a failure

  • Ingest the lab's scan data and reproduce the failure in simulation
  • Isolate the coupling path — it is rarely where the trace peaks
  • Design a targeted mitigation: snubber, shielding, ferrite selection, layout change
  • Verify the fix in the model before you pay for another lab slot

To be explicit: CEM LLC is not an accredited test laboratory and does not issue certifications. Formal FCC and CE testing happens at an accredited facility. We work alongside yours.

Tier 1 · roadmap Not available yet

Automated pre-flight PCB check

The long-term product: upload a board, get an automated read on the common EMI and SI traps without an engineer in the loop. Here is the intended flow, and an honest account of why it is not shipping today.

FLOW 01

Upload & parse

Drag and drop Gerber, ODB++, or IPC-2581 plus your stackup file. No geometry CAD, no manual meshing.

FLOW 02

Select the target standard

Answer questions in your language — "FCC Class B?", "which interfaces need SI review?" — instead of setting boundary conditions.

FLOW 03

Automated cloud solve

Rule-based checks plus full-wave solves on auto-scaled compute. Nothing for you to provision or license.

FLOW 04

Actionable report

The same plain-English Pass / Risk / Fail output the consulting engagements produce — "move decoupling capacitor C12 within 2 mm of U4 pin 8."

Why we haven't shipped it

Fully automated, zero-engineer-in-the-loop verdicts on arbitrary board geometry is the hardest unsolved problem in this space — it is most of what Ansys and Cadence still have not automated after decades of trying. Anyone promising it as a day-one product is understating both the engineering lift and the liability of a false "Pass" that a startup ships on.

What gets us there

Volume of validated cases. Every Tier 2 and Tier 3 engagement produces a matched pair — what the model said and what the board actually did — and that dataset is what makes an automated verdict defensible. The consulting is not a detour around the product. It is how the product gets built.
