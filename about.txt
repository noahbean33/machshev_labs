Frequently Asked Questions
What does "turnkey engineering design" include?
Complete project management from initial concept to final validated design. This includes CAD preparation, simulation setup, analysis, optimization, and comprehensive reporting with actionable recommendations.

Can you handle both simple and complex projects?
Absolutely. We scale our approach to match your needs — from quick design validation studies to complex multi-physics optimization challenges involving custom materials, advanced geometries, and sophisticated analysis requirements.

What kind of custom software do you develop?
We create parametric design tools, automated simulation workflows, custom post-processing scripts, and complete simulation pipelines tailored to your specific engineering process and requirements.

How does knowledge transfer work?
We offer hands-on training sessions, best practices workshops, mentoring programs, and ongoing consulting to help your team develop internal electromagnetic simulation capabilities and become self-sufficient.

What file formats do you work with?
We accept STEP, STL, IGES, and most common CAD formats. We can also clean up geometry, repair non-manifold edges, or simplify models for simulation as needed.

What's included in your engineering reports?
Professional PDF reports with simulation methodology, key performance metrics, annotated field visualizations, design recommendations, and expert analysis. Raw data and simulation files available upon request.

How do you determine project scope and approach?
We start with a detailed discussion of your requirements, objectives, and constraints. Based on this, we recommend the most appropriate service approach and provide a clear scope and timeline.
