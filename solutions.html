Whatever the band or the standard, the pattern repeats: models that cannot be tested and tests that cannot be modelled. Here is how four kinds of teams close it.

Radar & electronic warfare

Fly the threat scenario before the aircraft does

Build a dense, dynamic emitter environment in Studio, then play it straight into the receiver with Range. Your ESM library gets exercised against the same scene every night instead of twice a year on the range.

  • Pulse-agile emitters with realistic PRI stagger, hopping, and scan patterns
  • Terrain-masked line of sight and multipath against a moving platform
  • Jamming and counter-jamming loops with closed-loop technique response
  • Repeat a field anomaly on the bench from the recorded scenario hash

5G, 6G & non-terrestrial networks

Conformance as a build step, not a phase

Encode the 3GPP limit lines once and let CI enforce them. Bench runs the same sweeps on the simulator for every merge and on the chamber rack before every release candidate.

  • TR 38.901 UMa, UMi, RMa, and InH channel models with correlated fading
  • FR1 and FR2 measurement suites: EVM, ACLR, spurious, sensitivity, blocking
  • NTN Doppler and delay profiles from real LEO and GEO ephemerides
  • Beam-management sweeps across codebooks without touching the positioner
Satcom & ground segment

Model the pass, not just the link

Propagate real orbits, apply rain fade and scintillation from ITU-R models, and see what the terminal actually experiences across a whole pass — including handover between spacecraft.

  • TLE and SP3 ephemeris import with full geometry over time
  • ITU-R P.618 rain fade, gaseous absorption, and tropospheric scintillation
  • Ka- and V-band link budgets with adaptive coding and modulation loops
  • Terminal tracking error injection to bound pointing-loss margin

pa_characterisation.py 
from kestrel import bench, dut

pa = dut.Amplifier("KX-2740", band=(3.3e9, 4.2e9))

corners = bench.Matrix(
    temp_c   = [-40, 25, 85, 125],
    vdd      = [4.6, 5.0, 5.4],
    tone     = ["cw", "nr_100mhz", "two_tone"],
)

result = corners.characterise(
    pa, measure=["gain", "p1db", "pae", "am_pm"],
    chamber="thermal-3", settle_s=90,
)

result.to_datasheet("KX-2740_rev_c.pdf")
# 36 corners · 4h 12m unattended · 0 operator touches
Semiconductor & component

Characterise every corner without an operator

Device characterisation is a combinatorial problem that people should not be running by hand. Bench drives the chamber, the supplies, and the analyzers overnight, and produces the datasheet plots as an artifact.

  • Temperature, bias, and waveform corner matrices with automatic settling
  • Load-pull integration and X-parameter capture on supported VNAs
  • Golden-unit comparison to catch process drift between wafer lots
  • Datasheet-ready plots and tables generated straight from the run record
